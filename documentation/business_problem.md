# Business Problem

**Problem:** How can market participants, policymakers, or large energy
users track and reduce carbon intensity in the NEM without increasing
system costs?

Carbon intensity in the NEM varies dramatically by region and hour.
This project identifies which regions spike most, which fuel types are
responsible, and when — giving retailers and policymakers a
data-driven basis for intervention.

## Core Analytical Questions
1. How does carbon intensity change over time by NEM region?
2. Which fuel types drive carbon intensity spikes?
3. Are there system-level patterns (e.g. high demand → higher emissions)?
4. Can we identify low-carbon dispatch windows?
5. How can aggregation reduce query cost while preserving insight?
